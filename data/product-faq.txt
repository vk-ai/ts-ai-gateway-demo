Product FAQ

Q: What warranty do products include?
A: Most electronics include a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Wear-and-tear and accidental damage are excluded.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: After shipment you receive a tracking number by email. Tracking updates appear within 24 hours of carrier scan.

Q: Can I change my shipping address?
A: Address changes are allowed only before the order ships. Contact support as soon as possible with your order number.

Q: Do you price match?
A: We price match identical in-stock items from major national retailers within 7 days of purchase. Gift cards and marketplace third-party sellers are excluded.
