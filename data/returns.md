# Returns & Refunds

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery in unused condition with original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.
Opened software, gift cards, and clearance items marked final sale are not eligible for return.
To start a return, use the order confirmation email link or contact support with your order number.
Damaged or defective items qualify for a prepaid return label at no cost to the customer.
