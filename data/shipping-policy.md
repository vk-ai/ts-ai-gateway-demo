# Shipping Policy

Orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time ship the same business day.
Standard ground shipping takes 3–5 business days within the contiguous United States.
Expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee.
Free shipping applies to orders over $50 subtotal before tax.
International shipping is limited to Canada and Mexico; customs fees are the buyer's responsibility.
PO Box destinations receive USPS delivery only.
